  1. On iPad: open this page in the free Bluefy app (Safari can't use Bluetooth).
  2. On a Windows laptop: open it in Chrome or Edge.
  1. Close the Bosch MeasureOn app (the laser connects to one app at a time).
  2. Turn on the laser and press its Bluetooth button.
  3. Tap Connect laser and pick the GLM in the list.
  4. Press the measure button on the laser.
